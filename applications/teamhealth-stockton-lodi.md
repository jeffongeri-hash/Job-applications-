## Heads-up

Web search results said TeamHealth ED roles typically want **a minimum of two years of
emergency medicine experience**, while some sites offer extended onboarding for newer
clinicians. You have about one year. Ask the recruiter directly before investing in a long
application. The note below does that up front.

## Short recruiter note (email or portal message)

> Subject: PA-C – ED Advanced Practice Clinician, [Stockton / Lodi]
>
> Hello [Recruiter],
>
> I am a PA-C in the Emergency Department at UnityPoint Health – Allen Hospital, where I
> have worked since October 2025, so I have about one year of ED experience. I am
> interested in the [Stockton / Lodi] Emergency Medicine Advanced Practice Clinician role.
>
> Two quick questions before I apply: is one year of ED experience enough for this
> position, and does the site offer an onboarding or mentorship period? My California PA
> license application is in progress, with an expected issue date of [date].
>
> Thank you,
> [Name], PA-C · [Phone] · [Email]
