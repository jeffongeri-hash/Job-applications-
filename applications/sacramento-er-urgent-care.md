## Role-specific edits

- **Talent Solution Partners (evening shift):** add a sentence on night/evening ED experience, if true.
- **Vituity:** the posting describes a 54-bed ED with a separate pediatric ED and fast track. Reference the part that matches your own experience; do not claim pediatric experience you do not have.
- **Sutter:** use the Sutter Health application portal. Their forms ask for license details, so wait until the CA license date is firm.
- **All Inclusive (urgent care):** emphasise breadth: minor trauma, laceration repair, fracture care, point-of-care testing, only where it is true for you.
- **Medix / Jordan Search:** these are recruiters. Ask which employers and specialties are open, and for pay structure (W-2 vs 1099).
